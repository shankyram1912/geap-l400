# Analyze Flood Risk Skill

When evaluating environmental or geographic risks for a lot:
1. Check the `flood_zone` field from the property data.
2. Identify whether the lot is in a high-risk zone or minimal risk zone.
3. Note the general flood classification in your investigation notes.
